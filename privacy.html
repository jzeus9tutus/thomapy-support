Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Thomapy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Thomapy application for Apple Watch.

Information We Collect

Thomapy does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store personal information on external servers.

Financial Data

Expense records, budgets, subscriptions, categories, notes, and statistics created in Thomapy are stored locally on your device and are used only to provide the app's functionality.

Thomapy does not transmit this financial information to the developer or to third parties.

Accounts and Login

Thomapy does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal identification information.

Analytics and Advertising

Thomapy does not use third-party advertising networks, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics services.

Third-Party Services

Thomapy does not sell or share user data with data brokers, advertising companies, or other third parties.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device for as long as it is stored within the app. Removing app data or uninstalling Thomapy may permanently remove locally stored information.

Children's Privacy

Thomapy does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Thomapy's features or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.
