About Thomapy

Thomapy is a lightweight personal finance app designed exclusively for Apple Watch. Track your expenses in seconds directly from your wrist, analyze your spending habits through intuitive statistics, and stay in control of your finances without needing your iPhone.

Designed exclusively for Apple Watch

Features

  • ⌚ Fast expense tracking from Apple Watch.
  • 📊 Beautiful statistics to understand your spending.
  • 💰 Daily and monthly budget configuration.
  • 📅 Monthly subscription overview.
  • ⚡ Lightweight, simple and distraction-free.
  • 🎨 Modern interface designed for watchOS.

Privacy

Thomapy stores your financial information locally on your Apple Watch. No personal information is collected, shared or sold. The app does not require an account and does not use third-party analytics.
